Creamy Chicken Alfredo with Broccoli

Servings: 4 · Total time: PT35M

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb boneless skinless chicken breasts, diced
  • 12 oz dry fettuccine
  • 1 lb broccoli florets
  • 2 cups heavy cream
  • 1 cup grated Parmesan
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and black pepper

Directions

  1. Boil pasta until al dente; reserve 1/2 cup pasta water. Steam broccoli until just tender.
  2. Season chicken and cook in olive oil in a large skillet until browned and 165°F in the center; transfer to a plate.
  3. Reduce heat. Melt butter in skillet; stir in garlic for 30 seconds. Add cream and gently simmer 3–5 minutes (do not boil hard).
  4. Stir in Parmesan until smooth. Add chicken, pasta and broccoli; toss. Loosen with a little reserved pasta water as needed.

Leftover lunch: Refrigerate one serving for Wednesday; add a splash of milk when reheating.
