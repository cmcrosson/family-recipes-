Slow Cooker Beef Tips & Gravy with Mashed Potatoes

Servings: 4 · Total time: PT8H25M

Ingredients

  • 2 lb chuck roast, cut into 1 1/2-inch cubes (or beef stew meat)
  • 2 cups verified onion-free beef broth
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 2 tbsp cornstarch
  • 2 tbsp cold water
  • 2 lb Yukon Gold potatoes
  • 4 tbsp butter
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • Salt to taste

Directions

  1. In the morning, put beef, broth, garlic, thyme and pepper in slow cooker. Cover; cook on LOW 6–8 hours until tender.
  2. About 30 minutes before dinner, cut potatoes into chunks; boil in salted water 15–20 minutes until fork-tender. Drain and mash with butter and milk.
  3. Mix cornstarch and cold water; stir into beef juices. Cover and cook on HIGH 15–20 minutes until gravy thickens. Season to taste.
  4. Serve beef and gravy over mashed potatoes.

Leftover lunch: Pack one serving for Thursday. If away longer than the cook time, use a food-safe programmable warm setting.
