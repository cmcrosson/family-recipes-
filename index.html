Garlic Butter Chicken, Potatoes & Green Beans

4 servings · Oven · About 45 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 lb boneless skinless chicken breasts
  • 1 1/2 lb baby potatoes, halved
  • 1 lb green beans, trimmed
  • 4 tbsp butter
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp onion-free Italian seasoning
  • Salt and black pepper

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Toss potatoes with 1 tbsp olive oil, paprika, salt and pepper; spread on a sheet pan. Roast 15 minutes.
  2. Melt butter and stir in garlic and Italian seasoning. Season chicken; place in a separate baking dish and brush with garlic butter.
  3. Bake chicken about 20–25 minutes, until the thickest part reaches 165°F. Cooking time varies with thickness.
  4. Toss green beans with remaining 1 tbsp olive oil and add to potato pan for the last 12–15 minutes. Roast until vegetables are tender.

Leftover lunch

Pack one portion of chicken, potatoes and beans for Monday.

Check seasoning ingredients for onion or onion powder.
