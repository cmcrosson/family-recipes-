Honey Garlic Chicken, Rice & Carrots

Servings: 4 · Total time: PT40M

Ingredients

  • 2 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs or breasts, bite-sized
  • 1 1/2 cups dry white rice
  • 1 lb carrots, peeled and sliced
  • 1/3 cup honey
  • 1/4 cup low-sodium soy sauce (check for onion ingredients)
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch
  • 2 tbsp water
  • Salt and black pepper

Directions

  1. Cook rice according to package directions.
  2. Toss carrots with olive oil, salt and pepper. Air fry at 380°F for 12–18 minutes, shaking halfway, until tender.
  3. Season chicken lightly; air fry in a single layer at 380°F for about 12–16 minutes, shaking halfway. Cook in batches if needed; verify 165°F internally.
  4. In a saucepan, gently simmer honey, soy sauce and garlic. Stir cornstarch into water, add to sauce, and simmer until glossy and thickened.
  5. Toss cooked chicken with sauce; serve over rice alongside carrots.

Leftover lunch: Pack chicken, rice and carrots for Friday; refrigerate promptly.
