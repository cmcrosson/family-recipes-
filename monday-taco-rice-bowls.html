Cheesy Beef Taco Rice Bowls

Servings: 4 · Total time: PT30M

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb ground beef
  • 1 1/2 cups dry white rice
  • 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 cup frozen corn
  • 1 1/2 cups shredded Mexican-blend cheese
  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • 1 small head romaine lettuce, chopped
  • 1 tomato, diced (optional)
  • 2 tsp mild chili powder
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp oregano
  • 1/2 tsp salt

Directions

  1. Cook rice according to package directions.
  2. Brown beef in a skillet, breaking it up; drain excess fat.
  3. Stir in homemade seasoning and 1/2 cup water. Simmer 5 minutes. Add beans and corn; heat another 5 minutes.
  4. Divide rice and beef mixture into 4 bowls; top dinner portions with cheese, lettuce, tomato and sour cream.

Leftover lunch: Store lunch rice/beef separately from lettuce and sour cream; assemble Tuesday.
